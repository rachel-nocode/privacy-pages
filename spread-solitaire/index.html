Spread Solitaire

Privacy Policy.

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Spread Solitaire collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. Your games, stats and settings stay on your device.

Data we collect

None. Spread Solitaire does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information or usage data. The app makes no network connections of its own, so there is nothing for us to receive. We have no backend and no database of players.

Data stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the app saves a few things inside its own private storage on your iPhone:

  • Your game in progress: the cards on the table and your moves, so you can undo and resume after closing the app.
  • Your game history: for each finished or abandoned game, the deal number, draw mode, whether you won, moves, time and the date it ended. This is what your stats and streaks are worked out from.
  • Your settings: draw mode, winnable deals, tap to move, timer, left- or right-hand layout, four-colour suits, card back, haptics, and which tips you have already seen.

We cannot see any of this. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, these files are included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own backup settings, not by us.

Purchases

Spread Solitaire is a one-time paid download from the App Store. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple handles payment. We do not receive your name, email or payment details, only sales totals from Apple.

Third-party services

None. The app uses only Apple's own system frameworks. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no social or sign-in services. The typeface it uses (Fraunces, under the SIL Open Font License) is a file bundled inside the app and never contacts anyone.

If you have chosen in Settings on your iPhone to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage reports with us. That sharing is controlled by you in Settings and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Spread Solitaire is a general-audience card game and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released. If a change would mean collecting data, the App Store privacy label will be updated too.
