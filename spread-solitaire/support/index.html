Frequently asked

How do I play?

Build the four foundations up by suit from Ace to King. On the table, stack cards down in alternating colours. You can move a run of face-up cards together, and only a King can start an empty column. When the deck runs out, tap it to turn the waste back over. The full rules are under Menu → How to play.

How do I move cards?

Drag a card, or a run of cards, to where you want it. Or tap a card and it goes to the spot where it fits. If you prefer to choose, turn off "Tap to move" in Settings: then a tap selects a card and a second tap places it.

What does "Winnable deals only" mean?

Every deal in that list has been checked by a solver that can see all the cards, and it found a way to win. Some cards are face down while you play, so a winnable deal can still beat you. Turn the setting off for fully random deals, some of which can't be won.

What is Today's deal?

One winnable deal per day, picked from the date, so everyone playing gets the same deal. It works offline. Win it on consecutive days to build your daily streak.

I'm stuck. Is there a hint?

Yes. Open the Menu and tap "Show a move". Undo is always available too, and you can undo as far back as you like.

How does it work on iPhone Duo?

Fold it partway, like a book, and the table splits into two pages with nothing on the fold. Open it flat for one full board. Stand it on a table folded across the middle and the timer and foundations sit on the upright half. Close it and the game pauses and saves; the outer display shows your game with a Continue button, and you can keep playing there or open it up again.

Does it work on any iPhone?

Yes. Every feature works on any iPhone that runs the app. You get the classic layout in portrait and a wide board in landscape. Leaving the app pauses your game and saves it.

Will I lose my game if I close the app?

No. The game saves after every move. Open the app again and tap Continue.

Can I play left-handed?

Yes. In Settings, set Hand to Left. The deck, buttons and first three columns move to the other side.

My stats are gone after I deleted the app.

All data lives only on your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it. We don't keep a copy, so we can't restore it. Restoring your iPhone from a backup made before you deleted the app brings it back.

I got a new iPhone. Do I have to pay again?

No. Sign in with the same Apple Account and download Spread Solitaire again from your purchases in the App Store at no extra cost.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. You pay once in the App Store and that's it. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases, no account.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.
