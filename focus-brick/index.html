Privacy Policy — Focus Brick

Effective date: 2026-06-21

Summary

Focus Brick is a privacy-friendly focus app. Your bricks, tasks, preferences, and progress stay on your device.

Data we collect

Focus Brick does not collect personal data, does not require an account, and does not transmit user-generated content to external servers.

Data stored on your device

Your tasks, completed bricks, app preferences, onboarding state, and focus history are stored locally using Apple's on-device frameworks such as SwiftData and UserDefaults. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Third-party services

Focus Brick uses Apple StoreKit for Pro subscription purchases and restore flow. Subscription billing and purchase history are handled by Apple. Focus Brick does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Focus Brick is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The effective date will reflect changes.
